## Direction contract

THESIS: All four life areas stand together as test tubes in one white lab rack, each filled with its area's color to exactly its score. Fullness is read visually: no step labels and no per-level names, by the owner's explicit request. This replaces the category default of a 2×2 card grid with ring gauges.

OWN-WORLD: A cool pale wall (#E9ECEE) carries flat white objects with soft shadows: the rack's top bar, two posts and the base. Four ten-step OKLCH ladders, which the owner approved: Health green, Work blue, Play marigold, Love coral. Liquid is a step 5→7 gradient with a step-4 back wave. The empty part of each tube is a 14% wash of step 4. Tubes have a grey wall and lip, a white shine streak, and slow white bubbles. A round black "Start here" sticker marks the lowest area. Type is Bricolage Grotesque, self-hosted with its opsz and wdth axes: heavy display, tabular scores, small-caps note heads with a color swatch.

STORY: The visitor sees four levels side by side, the lowest one flagged, then reads each area's note on why it got that score.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The title "Start Where You Are" is top left, with the lede, the summary line (most room to grow, strongest), and, only while content is placeholder, a placeholder chip on the right.
